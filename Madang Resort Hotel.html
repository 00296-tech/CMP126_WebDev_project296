Welcome to Madang Resort

Family-owned & operated, the Madang Resort has been providing warm hospitality and unforgettable experiences to guests for over 45 years.

Nestled amidst lush tropical gardens in the picturesque coastal town of Madang, Papua New Guinea, our charming and affordable resort is the perfect place to escape the hustle and bustle of city life and immerse yourself in the natural beauty of the region.

  • Conferences & Events

    We understand the importance of hosting successful conferences and events. Our resort offers modern facilities and unparalleled service to ensure that your event is a success.

    With a variety of conference rooms and outdoor event spaces, we can accommodate events up to 300 people. Our resort also offers catering services, audio-visual equipment, and high-speed internet access to meet all of your conference and event needs.

    See More

  • Accommodation

    We are dedicated to providing a diverse range of comfortable and stylish accommodations, designed to suit the needs and preferences of all types of travellers.

    From our cozy standard rooms to our presidential suites, every room offers stunning views of the surrounding landscape, with some even offering breathtaking ocean views.

    See More

  • Dining

    At Madang Resort, we pride ourselves on using fresh, locally sourced ingredients in our dishes.

    Our chefs create unique and authentic dishes that highlight the flavours of Papua New Guinea, as well as a range of hot and cold beverages. Whether you're relaxing by the pool or dining in our restaurant, our attentive staff are committed to ensuring that your experience is exceptional.
